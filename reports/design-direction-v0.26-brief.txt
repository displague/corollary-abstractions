You are an outside advisor to a small research program you have never seen.
Everything you know about it is in this message; no code or data will be
provided. Your value here is precisely that you are outside it.

The program is building an inspectable system that lets a person work across
mathematics, logic, language, physical relations, and programming without
hiding exact operations inside a large learned model. It holds a generated
library of formal statements, exact matchers and checkers, scoped hypothetical
worlds, a persistent conversation, and a small amount of learned machinery for
the residual choices that cannot be decided exactly. A person should eventually
be able to speak naturally, introduce assumptions, obtain conditional answers,
ask the running environment for observations, and continue a conversation whose
claims remain auditable.

Where it stands. The library already contains far more relations than a person
can name or reach: only a small named fraction can currently be requested, and
the bulk shares a handful of boilerplate glossary words. The conversation can
remember assumptions, pause for missing information over a signed question
channel, and resume exactly. It can render a bounded formal subset into checked
English and evaluate some pinned numeric substitutions. It takes a small set of
ordinary conversational turns -- a greeting, a liveness reply, a quoted datum,
an expected program output -- as verified interaction moves on a served
surface: the same short utterance takes different moves depending on exact
session context and exact vocabulary evidence, and when more than one
materially different move survives, the system pauses and asks instead of
guessing. That move vocabulary is small and authored by the program itself, and
conformance to its transition rules does not establish that the rules model
human convention.

Most recently the person gained a small authoring power of their own. A
declaration line admits one fresh relation symbol -- a name, a number of
argument places, and a category for each place -- into a session-scoped
register, or refuses it with exactly one deciding clause, totally and by
default toward refusal. From that turn on, a supposition applying the declared
symbol with the wrong number of arguments is refused by name, where yesterday
it was held as opaque text. Nothing declared survives the session, reaches a
generated library file, or carries any claim of truth or usefulness; rules,
axioms and premises ABOUT a declared symbol are refused in writing by the same
function that admits the symbol. What premise grammar real declarations would
need is the program's own recorded next question, and there is as yet no corpus
of real declarations to price it against.

The system still does not invoke general programming work, use environmental
observations as live input, certify which assumptions an answer actually
needed, read ordinary English as input rather than only writing it, or let a
person reach the unnamed bulk of the library. An attempt to bridge its
pause-and-ask channel into an existing command-line chat client's structured
prompting tool failed honestly and is recorded: the client advertises a
prompting tool its own policy refuses to execute in the mode people actually
use, and a plain-text pause remains the working path.

Measurement culture. Every new behavior must produce a first-class inspectable
artifact, a capability-blind control, corruption and vacuity checks, gates
frozen in writing before any measurement, honest negative results, and a
smallest slice one maintainer can finish inside one release cycle. Three
failures of that culture were found by adversarial review in the last cycle,
after all its gates had been reported green, and they are worth knowing because
they shape what counts here as a good proposal:

1. A capability-blind control was scored green. The review then computed the
   best score any member of the registered blind family could reach on the
   scored half; it fell below the voiding threshold. No member could have
   fired. Comparing a control family's own ceiling against its threshold is a
   one-line computation, and nobody had done it before the score existed.
2. A containment gate reported that thirty-two seeded attacks were all
   stopped. The attacks were prose DESCRIPTIONS of attempts; nothing in the
   program executes one. What was actually observed is that a detector fires,
   which is a fact about the detector.
3. A value drawn from a sealed corpus was published inside the system's own
   documentation of its command grammar, and every generated artifact echoing
   that documentation copied it. A containment check scoped to a run's outputs
   cannot see anything already committed.

Standing constraints. Exact work must remain ordinary code: parsing, equality,
scope, provenance, authorization, resource limits, process results, status, and
durable receipts. A small learned component may rank or compose only among
legal alternatives; it cannot declare truth, authorize side effects,
manufacture missing user input, or turn an observation into a library fact.
Generated library files come only from committed sources. There is one
maintainer and no user population, so any direction that needs outside
participants must degrade honestly to "untested" rather than block a cycle.
More library entries, more matches, or placing a model on an exactly computable
operation are suspended proxies, not directions.

Propose FIVE distinct forward directions. For each, in under 150 words:
(a) the new kind of claim it would make true; (b) why the current shape
cannot make that claim today; (c) a falsifiable construction gate -- what,
decided in advance, would count as the idea failing; (d) the cheapest honest
failure. Do not propose more entries, more matches, or a model trained on an
exactly computable capability. Range widely; at least one should feel risky to
you. The five must not be five flavors of one idea. Number them with short
memorable names.
