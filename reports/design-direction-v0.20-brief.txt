You are an outside advisor to a small research program you have never
seen. Everything you know about it is in this message; no code or data
will be provided. Your value here is precisely that you are outside it.

The program's thesis: everything with a closed form belongs in
inspectable code, with learned components carrying only a graded
residual — and never the difference between refusing and answering. It
maintains a graph of about thirteen thousand exact statements across
twenty-seven subject areas, regenerating byte-identically from
committed seed programs; structural ledgers find repeated skeletons
across fields; a typed session answers with receipts, refuses what it
cannot ground, and asks signed clarifying questions — served at a
terminal and over the standard chat-completions HTTP API that coding
agents attach to.

Its last three cycles, each gate-adjudicated and published with
receipts, reds included:
- It measured itself against a small language model holding the very
  same records in context: correct receipted answers at hundreds of
  times the model's effective throughput; the model, told to quote,
  could not reproduce exact content at speed.
- It gave its statements a voice: formal terms render into English
  sentences gated by re-parsing each back to the exact structure it
  claims — 99.9 percent round-trip — then two glyph substitutions
  turned out to unlock half the corpus (voice now covers two-thirds),
  because half the "unreadable" layer was an alphabet problem, not a
  grammar problem.
- And this cycle it published three honest non-wins alongside: a
  register of what cannot be said and why; a rendering gate VOIDED by
  its own blind control (deleting a redundant bracket changes the
  sentence but not the term, and the gate cannot see the difference —
  now a measured boundary, not a hope); a maintainer-loved indexing
  idea adopted, built, measured against pre-registered baselines, and
  parked BY the numbers when it lost to the existing channel; and a
  census showing its corpora contain notational forks only — the
  authors never disagreed about mathematics, only spelling.

The graph grows at runtime, append-only, through a proven-write gate.
Measurement culture, all binding: preregistration before
implementation; gates frozen before numbers; capability-blind controls
with written voiding sentences; negatives published as first-class;
receipts for failed runs. Constraints: one maintainer; a first slice
fits one release cycle and yields a committed checkable artifact; no
multi-hour training; learned components may rank among licensed
candidates but never author structure or unlock an answer.

The recurring failure mode this program polices in itself: headline
drift toward instruments about its own evidence. Its standing product
ambition: a person or agent converses with exact knowledge — asks,
gets receipts at wire speed, watches the graph grow as knowledge is
encountered — with small models handling only what is best left to
small models.

Propose FIVE distinct forward directions. For each, in under 150
words: (a) the new kind of claim it would make true; (b) why the
current shape cannot make that claim today; (c) a falsifiable
construction gate — what, decided in advance, would count as the idea
