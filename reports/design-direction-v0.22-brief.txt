You are an outside advisor to a small research program you have never seen.
Everything you know about it is in this message; no code or data will be
provided. Your value here is precisely that you are outside it.

THE PROGRAM'S THESIS. Small language models are poor at being right and
programs are poor at being broad. The program bets on a third shape: a
large, growing library of exact machine-checkable statements (currently
mathematics), plus ordinary programs that parse, index, evaluate, and
answer over that library, plus -- at the edges only -- small learned
components that may PROPOSE but never DECIDE. Every served answer must be
a quotation, a computation, or a refusal, each carrying a receipt a
stranger can re-check offline. An external proof checker the program did
not build adjudicates formal claims; its verdicts are pinned and
replayable.

WHAT EXISTS TODAY (by kind, not name):
- A statement library of ~12,800 exact statements across ~27 topic areas,
  ingested from public formal-math sources; it grows at runtime through a
  gated write path that accepts only machine-proven material, append-only.
- A chat-style HTTP service speaking a common LLM API, so third-party
  agent harnesses can attach. Input today is a registered grammar of typed
  one-line commands, not open prose. A capability sheet teaches the
  grammar.
- A measured throughput result: for questions inside the registered
  grammar over covered ground, the system serves correct receipted answers
  hundreds of times faster than a pinned small-LLM baseline; three voiding
  controls guard the claim.
- Formal statements can be rendered as verified English sentences:
  rendering is licensed only by an exact round trip back through the proof
  checker; about two-thirds of the library speaks natively this way; a
  second English voice for statements that cannot round-trip natively
  passed its verification gates this cycle after failing them last cycle,
  and is now served.
- Statements can be COMPILED to exact evaluators (integers and exact
  rationals; typed number domains; refusal on anything outside the
  declared reading), and a served route returns per-point verdicts with
  receipts. But the registered verification run for "the library conforms
  at sampled points" VOIDED on its own controls: one control's floor
  turned out unmeetable even by a correct instrument over restricted
  number domains, and an independent-adjudication control turned out not
  to have executed on the class that mattered. No conformance rate is
  claimed. The route serves with the void published on it.
- Refusals are typed and named everywhere; a refusal is a first-class
  result. Falsification-only honesty is the rule: finding no
  counterexample certifies nothing universal.
- A blind-reader instrument exists: a pinned local model that grades
  whether served sentences carry meaning to a reader who cannot see the
  formal source. Its own control voided the reader-claim this cycle
  (scrambled sentences still leak structure), so no reader claim is made
  -- the instrument survives, the claim does not.

MEASUREMENT CULTURE. Preregistration before implementation: gates and
floors frozen before numbers exist; capability-blind baselines with
written voiding sentences; negative results and voids published
first-class (three of the last five cycles led with one); every claim's
denominator sealed before the scored party can move it. The recurring
failure mode the program has learned to hunt in itself: assertions
written so they cannot go red -- checks that compare a thing to itself,
prose that carries authority beside machine-checked numbers without being
machine-checked, controls whose perfect score would be meaningless.

STANDING CONSTRAINTS:
- Exact code owns structure, equality, and verdicts. A learned component
  may rank, propose, or translate INTO the exact layer, and everything it
  proposes must be verified by exact code before it can matter.
- One maintainer, one workstation, pinned local models and toolchains, no
  network fetches at run time. First slices must fit one release cycle
  and produce a committed, checkable artifact.
- Outside participation (volunteers, strangers, crowds) must degrade
  honestly to "untested" -- it may not block a cycle.
- The library may only grow by addition; nothing already committed is
  rewritten by growth.

AMBITIONS (the boundary the program wants moved, stated as human
abilities):
- A person should eventually hold a plain conversation with it, in plain
  text, and get only answers that are exact, receipted, or honestly
  refused -- including answers conditional on stated assumptions when the
  question was ambiguous.
- The system should know and say what it does not know, and make its
  ignorance useful: what would need to be true, what is missing, what
  distinguishes the candidate readings.
- The library's unreachable majority should become reachable: most of it
  cannot yet be answered about, evaluated, or spoken.
- Downstream software agents should be able to trust and compose its
  answers without re-verifying them.

DO NOT PROPOSE: more corpus entries as the headline; more matches or
counts as the result; training a model to imitate an exact capability; a
rate-only deliverable; benchmarks against large LLMs.

SINCE THE LAST TIME THIS BRIEF WAS WRITTEN (one release ago), four
things changed, stated plainly because they move what is worth
proposing:
- The SESSION now exists as a first-class object: a person's declared
  assumptions persist across turns as receipted records, every answer
  names which assumptions it consumed, and a stranger can replay a
  recorded conversation offline and get the same bytes or a typed
  refusal. Gated by mutation controls (sham and uncited assumptions
  must move nothing -- zero flips measured), a keyed tamper check, and
  a fence proving session state never leaks into unconditional
  answers.
- Plain-text intake was built and its verification run FAILED
  honestly, twice over. (1) The learned proposer (a small pinned
  model choosing an INDEX into an exactly-enumerated candidate list --
  it can never emit free text into the system) was beaten by the
  blind random arm on the frozen metric -- and the postmortem showed
  the metric structurally could not reward a correct refusal: every
  one of the model's decline-to-answer responses was verifiably
  correct, while the blind arm, which cannot decline, got credit for
  inventing. The frozen chance rate was mis-derived (1/N instead of
  from the near-free verification cost), so the floor was unmeetable
  by an honest instrument. (2) The system's OLD exact-match layer
  silently binds 13 of 30 naturally-phrased questions to a reading
  before the proposer is ever consulted, without recording that a
  choice happened -- measured, published, unrepaired, filed as a
  designed successor.
- A proof-carrying successor to the voided conformance claim stopped
  itself at its pilot: with one shared front-end, every
  statement-vs-evaluator agreement obligation reduced to P-iff-P, and
  the counterfactual showed the checker happily discharging six
  vacuous lemmas with the triviality trap removed. The lane parks
  behind an INDEPENDENT SECOND READING of each statement as a
  construction prerequisite.
- A recurring blocker got named twice in one cycle: candidate
  ENUMERATION and RANKING. One motivating question enumerated zero
  candidates (the corpus has the answer under a title sharing no
  words with the question); another buried its two correct readings
  at ranks 21 and 23 behind short irrelevant titles because the
  tiebreak was ascending title length. The synonym/naming layer
  between a person's words and the library's titles is now the
  measured bottleneck of the intake ambition.
