You are an external referee and research advisor to a small research
programme you have not seen. Everything you may know of it is contained
in this document; no code, data, or bibliography will be provided.
Your value lies precisely in your exteriority and in the divergence of
your priors from those of the programme's habitual reviewers. Please
respond in a scholarly register, with explicit argumentation: state
your premises, derive your claims, and mark conjecture as conjecture.

THE PROGRAMME'S THESIS. Small language models are unreliable assertors,
and conventional programs are narrow ones. The programme investigates a
third architecture: a large and growing library of exact,
machine-checkable statements (presently mathematics), served by
ordinary programs that parse, index, evaluate, and answer over that
library, with small learned components admitted only at the periphery
under a strict contract — they may PROPOSE, they may never DECIDE.
Every served answer is a quotation, a computation, or a refusal, each
carrying a receipt that a third party can re-verify offline. An
external proof assistant that the programme did not author adjudicates
formal claims; its verdicts are pinned by digest and replayable.

THE CURRENT STATE OF THE ARTIFACT, described by kind rather than name:
- A statement library of ~12,800 exact statements across ~27 topic
  areas, ingested from public formal-mathematics corpora, growing at
  runtime through a gated write path that admits only machine-proven
  material, append-only.
- A chat-style HTTP service conforming to a widely-adopted LLM API, so
  third-party agent harnesses can attach; input is a registered grammar
  of typed one-line commands; a capability sheet teaches the grammar.
- A measured throughput result: for questions within the registered
  grammar over covered ground, correct receipted answers are served
  several hundred times faster than a pinned small-LLM baseline, under
  three preregistered voiding controls.
- A conversation object: recorded sessions are committed, replayable
  artifacts; a declared assumption persists across turns as a receipted
  record; every answer names which assumptions it consumed; mutation
  controls (sham and uncited assumptions must move nothing) and keyed
  message-authentication chains gate the claim. The live prompt does
  not yet persist assumptions; only recorded sessions do, and the
  distinction is published rather than blurred.
- Verified English renderings of formal statements, licensed only by an
  exact round trip through the proof assistant; roughly two-thirds of
  the library speaks natively; a second voice for the formally foreign
  residue passed its verification gates after once failing them.
- A compiler from statements to exact evaluators (integers and exact
  rationals under declared domains), whose corpus-level conformance
  claim VOIDED on its own preregistered controls; the route serves
  per-point verdicts with the void published on it, and no conformance
  rate exists anywhere in the programme's literature.
- Three registered outcomes in the most recent cycle, with three
  distinct verdicts, all published with mechanism: the conversation
  object SERVED; plain-language intake FAILED HONESTLY (a
  capability-blind control outscored the learned proposer because the
  frozen metric was structurally incapable of rewarding a correct
  refusal — all six of the model's declinations were verifiably
  correct); and a proof-carrying conformance successor STOPPED ITSELF
  at its pilot upon discovering that a single shared front-end reduced
  every obligation to the tautology P-iff-P.
- A measured negative of consequence: the two committed non-title
  indices yield discriminating "handles" for roughly 2% of the
  library, because the bulk-ingested majority carries only boilerplate
  glossary entries. The working hypothesis for the coming cycle is
  that the ingested library is effectively NAMELESS, and that the
  naming layer between human vocabulary and formal content must be
  constructed rather than indexed.

THE MEASUREMENT CULTURE, which binds any proposal you make:
preregistration precedes implementation; gates and floors are frozen
before instruments exist, and every frozen floor must be accompanied at
registration by an argument that a correct instrument could meet it;
capability-blind baselines with written voiding sentences accompany
every claim; negative results and voids are published as first-class
outcomes; every metric must derive its chance rate from measured
verification cost rather than nominal alternatives (1/N is forbidden),
and every metric must be capable of rewarding a correct refusal. The
recurring failure mode the programme hunts in itself: assertions so
constructed that they cannot go red — checks that compare an object to
itself, prose that borrows authority from adjacent machine-checked
numbers, controls whose perfect score would be uninformative.

STANDING CONSTRAINTS: exact code owns structure, equality, and
verdicts; one maintainer, one workstation, pinned local models and
toolchains, no network access at serving time; first slices must fit
one release cycle and yield a committed, checkable artifact; outside
participation must degrade honestly to "untested" rather than block a
cycle; the library grows only by addition.

AMBITIONS, stated as human capabilities: a person holds a plain
conversation in plain text and receives only exact, receipted, or
honestly-refused answers, including answers conditional on stated
assumptions; the system knows and says what it does not know, and
renders its ignorance actionable; the unreachable majority of the
library becomes reachable; downstream software agents compose its
answers without re-verification.

EXCLUDED TERRITORY. The following conceptual ground is already claimed

(i) title-independent reachability via exactly-derived handle sets

(iv) composable receipts and void propagation across routes;
(v) typed-delta replay of served history against a grown library;

(vii) refusals carrying executed question-repairs; (viii) receipts
that survive deletion of the producing program; (ix) sequence-level

production-versus-recheck ratio on a named floor machine;
(xi) declared scaling classes frozen before measured growth, with the
decomposition of the throughput result into architectural and

(xiii) cost ceilings as typed refusals; (xiv) fault-injection scopes
for exactness on unfaithful hardware; (xv) person-taught macros over
