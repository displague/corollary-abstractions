# Roadmap v0.17 — the sentence is bound to its source, or it is not printed

v0.16 asked whether the blast radius of a retraction could be recovered
from finished prose, and the answer — delivered by the gate built to
give it — was no: the edges carry information, the precision does not
exist. The same cycle caught the front page quoting a number four
rewrites stale and caught the correction of that error being wrong
itself. Three findings, one lesson: lineage cannot be recovered after
the fact, so it must be paid at authoring time. This cycle pays it.

Governing design: [claims are emitted, not
written](DESIGN-ledger-first-claims.md) — chosen by the second full
outside course (three isolated series, fifteen directions, receipts in
`reports/design-direction-v0.17.json`), independently converged on by
all three series and the program's own void post-mortem, then hardened
by adversarial review before this roadmap existed.

## 1. Ledger-first claims (headline, architecture)

Implement DESIGN-ledger-first-claims in its registered order (§7): the

The gate is L1–L13 as frozen in the design: ≥90% conversion under the
sealed rule with every narrowing logged; 100% citation resolution with
loud document-blocking failure; linter recall 100/30-seeded and triage
≤60 recorded minutes; zero misses against the pre-committed audit; the
path-token baseline beaten outright; the recheck mutation-priced at
≥95% catch with a content-free-checker vacuity guard; shuffle-max
exceeded; stranger authorship of seeds and catalogue; one document, one
artifact, no retrospection; purity (records checkable without program
code); the <25-sentence exploratory floor. Two blind controls, either
able to void. Stop conditions include the design's own premise check:
more than three `not_a_function_of_artifact` narrowings in the first
ten conversions stops the cycle and publishes the finding.
